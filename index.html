4. FAQ

Common Questions

Answers to questions clients frequently ask.

01. What documents do I need for a property dispute case? +
You'll need the sale deed, Record of Rights (ROR), encumbrance certificate, any prior court orders, and relevant tax receipts. Bring whatever documents you have — we'll help identify what's missing during the consultation.
02. How long does civil litigation typically take in district court? +
Timelines vary depending on case complexity and court schedules. Simple suits may resolve in 12–18 months, while contested matters can take longer. We focus on strategic filings and strong documentation to minimize delays.
03. Can I check my case status online? +
Yes. Visit our Case Status Checker and enter your case number to view the current status, next hearing date, and recent updates — available 24/7.
04. What are the consultation fees? +
The initial consultation is charged at a nominal fee, which is adjusted against the final professional fees if you choose to engage us. Contact us or visit the office for specific fee details based on your case type.
05. Do you handle cases outside Anantapur district? +
Yes. We handle cases across Andhra Pradesh, including matters before the High Court, District Courts, and Revenue Tribunals in neighboring districts. Travel and appearance arrangements can be discussed during consultation.
06. What is ROR and why is it important for my land? +
The Record of Rights (ROR) is the official government record establishing land ownership, tenancy, and cultivation rights. It is critical evidence in any property dispute. Errors in ROR can affect your title — we specialize in ROR corrections and appeals.
