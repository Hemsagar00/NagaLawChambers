# Naga Law Chambers

Official website of **Advocate S. Nagendra Naik**, practicing at Anantapur Bar, Andhra Pradesh.

## About

Naga Law Chambers is a full-service legal practice serving citizens, farmers, and government job applicants across Anantapur district with expertise in:

- Revenue & Land Documentation
- Civil & Criminal Litigation
- Family & Property Disputes
- Legal Drafting & Consultation
